# User Management & Roles

The User Management module handles customer accounts, system users (staff), role-based access permissions, and billing balances.

---

## 1. Customers vs System Users

- **Customers**: Vehicle owners receiving tracking services and monthly bills. Customers can log in to view active vehicles, pay invoices via SSLCommerz, and submit support tickets.
- **System Users (Staff / Admins)**: Administrators and support staff responsible for managing customers, creating bills, processing payments, and updating system configurations.

---

## 2. Expiration Dates & Advance Balances

- **Account Expiration Date**: Set an account expiration date for customers. Expiration dates automatically update when bills are paid or extended manually.
- **Advance Payment Balance**: Customers can make advance payments. Advance balances are automatically applied when generating monthly invoices.

> [!WARNING]
> Expired accounts will trigger billing alerts if `Enable Bill Alert` is enabled for the customer.
