# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026  
**Software Name:** AsthaX CRM  
**Publisher:** AsthaX  

---

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide billing automation and vehicle management services, we collect:
- **Account Information:** Name, phone number, email address, billing address, and login credentials.
- **Telemetry & Device Data:** Vehicle identification numbers, GPSWOX server synchronization IDs, and device status.
- **Payment Telemetry:** Transaction reference IDs, invoice totals, and gateway payment logs (processed securely via SSLCommerz).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use collected data solely for:
- Generating monthly invoices, processing online payments, and recording transaction history.
- Dispatching automated SMS and email notifications (payment receipts, bill alerts, 2FA OTP codes).
- Synchronizing device status with GPSWOX tracking servers.
- Managing support tickets and system security audit logs.

---

## 3. Data Security & Protection
We employ enterprise-grade security controls including database password hashing (Bcrypt), encrypted sessions, Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), SSL/TLS transport encryption, and automated database backups to prevent unauthorized access or data loss.

---

## 4. Third-Party Integrations
We do not sell or monetize customer data. Data is shared exclusively with necessary operational integration providers:
- **SSLCommerz Gateway:** Secure online payment processing.
- **SMS Service Providers:** Delivering transactional SMS notifications.
- **GPSWOX Server API:** Synchronizing device expiration dates and active status.
