# Getting Started & Dashboard Overview

Welcome to **AsthaX GPSWOX CRM**. This platform provides complete control over vehicle tracking, automated monthly billing, user permissions, customer support ticketing, and system synchronization.

> [!NOTE]
> All primary CRM actions (Users, Vehicles, Bills, Payments, Support) are accessible from the main left sidebar navigation.

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard provides real-time operational metrics based on your user role:

- **Admin Metrics**: Total registered customers, active system users, total vehicles, monthly billing summaries, and quick action shortcuts.
- **Customer Overview**: Active vehicles, subscription expiration status, outstanding monthly bills, and payment links.

---

## 2. Security & Idle Screen Lock

To ensure system security, the CRM includes an automated **Screen Lock** feature:

- **Manual Lock**: Click the **Lock Screen** option in the user profile menu to lock your session instantly.
- **Idle Screen Lock**: The system will automatically lock the interface after a period of inactivity configured in your Profile Settings.
- **Unlocking**: Enter your account password to resume your session without losing your working state.

> [!TIP]
> You can configure your idle screen lock duration under **Profile Settings -> Security & Idle Lock**.
