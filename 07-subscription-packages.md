# Subscription Packages & Plans

The Subscription Packages module allows administrators to create standardized pricing tiers for vehicle tracking services and assign them to vehicles or customer accounts.

---

## 1. Overview of Subscription Packages

Subscription packages simplify monthly bill calculations by bundling vehicle tracking features and default rates:

- **Package Name**: E.g. Standard Bike Tracking, Car Premium Tracking, Heavy Commercial Fleet.
- **Monthly Fee / Price**: Standard monthly billing amount charged per assigned vehicle.
- **Package Description**: Features included in the package (e.g. 24/7 Engine Control, Geo-fence Alerts, Fuel Monitoring).
- **Active Status**: Enable or disable packages from being assigned to new vehicles.

---

## 2. Managing Subscription Packages

> [!NOTE]
> Administrators can manage subscription packages by navigating to **Settings -> Subscription Packages**.

### Steps to Create a New Package:
1. Click **New Subscription Package**.
2. Enter the **Package Name**, **Monthly Rate (BDT)**, and **Description**.
3. Click **Save Package**.

---

## 3. Assigning Packages to Vehicles

When adding or editing a vehicle record, select the appropriate **Subscription Package**. The vehicle's default monthly billing rate will automatically sync with the package rate upon monthly invoice generation.

> [!TIP]
> Custom vehicle rates can still be set individually if a customer has a negotiated custom rate that differs from the standard package.
