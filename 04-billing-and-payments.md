# Billing, Invoicing & SSLCommerz Payments

The Billing Engine automates monthly bill generation, invoice printing, discount management, and online payment processing.

---

## 1. Generating Monthly Bills

Bills can be generated automatically on a scheduled basis or manually triggered:
- Navigate to **Bills -> Generate Bills**.
- Select the target billing month and year.
- Click **Generate Bills** to run batch bill creation for all active vehicles.

---

## 2. Payments & Invoicing

- **Manual Payment**: Record manual cash, bKash, or bank transfers against customer bills.
- **SSLCommerz Online Payment**: Customers can click the **Pay Online** link to pay bills via credit card, mobile banking (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), or net banking.
- **PDF & Detailed Invoices**: Download itemized monthly PDF invoices or print single vehicle invoices directly.

> [!TIP]
> Shareable payment links can be sent via SMS or WhatsApp directly from the billing actions menu.
